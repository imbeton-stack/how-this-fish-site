Privacy Policy

For How This Fish (com.beton.howthisfish), a free mobile game published by Beton.

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Game data on your device

The game does not require an account. Game progress, earned coins, equipment, settings and language preferences are stored locally on your device. The game does not provide its own cloud account or cloud save service. Uninstalling the game or resetting its progress can remove this local information.

Advertising and third-party services

The game includes interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements, provided through Appodeal and its mediated advertising partners. The application also includes a consent management platform. Availability of networks and advertisements depends on configuration, region and consent.

Advertising and related technical services may collect and share the following information:

  • Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers, IP address, device model, operating system, language, network information and technical device signals.
  • Approximate location inferred from an IP address. The game does not request GPS location permissions.
  • Ad impressions, clicks and other interactions with advertising.
  • Diagnostics, crash information and performance signals used to operate, measure and improve the advertising services and prevent fraud.

These providers process information to deliver and measure advertisements, personalize ads where permitted, analyze service performance, and detect fraud or security problems. Data practices vary by provider and the permissions or consent you give. The game does not ask you to provide your name, email, payment details, contacts, photographs or messages for gameplay.

SDKs included in the Android application may provide services through Appodeal, Bidon, BidMachine, AppLovin, Mintegral, myTarget/VK Ads, Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize/Vungle, Yandex Ads and Sentry. A bundled SDK does not mean that every network displays ads in every session. The providers' policies describe their processing, retention and privacy rights. See Appodeal's Privacy Policy and its Android data disclosure documentation for information about Appodeal and mediated networks.

Your choices

When a consent form is required and made available by the advertising platform, it is shown through that platform. An advertising privacy entry is available in the game's Play settings when the consent platform reports it is required. You can also use your Android advertising privacy controls, including resetting or deleting the advertising ID where supported. Core gameplay can be used offline; ads need a network connection. Optional rewarded ads are started by your choice.

Retention and deletion

Local game progress remains on your device until you reset or remove it. Third-party advertising data is retained under each provider's policies and applicable law. To ask about access, correction, deletion or an objection to processing, contact us at fastmeshstudio@gmail.com. We can explain which provider may handle the request; we cannot directly erase information controlled by an independent advertising provider. Do not send passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us so we can investigate and address the request with the relevant provider.

Security and international processing

Third-party services may process information in countries other than yours. We do not promise that every advertising resource or transfer uses encryption. No system offers absolute security. Review each provider's privacy information for its safeguards and international transfer practices.

External links and policy updates

Advertisements and external links may lead to services governed by their own policies. We may update this page as the game's services change; the effective date above identifies the current policy.
